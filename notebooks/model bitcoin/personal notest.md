

### Leverage
Crypto exchanges unlike normal exchanges allow users up to 100x leverage
whilst regulated markets like etf's, bonds and stocks typically do not allow users 
more than 5-10x leverage and some prime brokers allow up to 20x on some exchanges, f.i tokyo and london.  

What results is cascading liquidation. Suppose we have a large number of highly leveraged long traders, a relatively small
move down would cause traders to liquidate their positions. The artificial selling of positions cause the price to drop even further
leading to cascading liquidation of the highly leveraged traders. 
A small price moved by btc acts a match to fire, greatly amplifying the small drop to an unnaturally large one.
